# AquaMap Laguna

A Laravel + MySQL GIS information system for a six-day school mini hackathon, focused publicly on **28 locations: 10 lakes, 5 river reaches and 13 waterfalls** in Laguna. The database has **30 records**, including two spring-associated resorts that remain off the public map but are available in category-first reporting under the user's newer explicit request. The 28 original records are unchanged; Balon-Balon Falls and Ambon-Ambon Falls were added through a guarded, repeatable targeted import.

The environmental refocus prioritizes aquatic species and habitat evidence, water body classification, and community-based visible pollution observations. Environmental summaries precede reference photographs. Public report information is limited to per-water-body totals and category counts of currently verified, unresolved observations; pending reports and individual details stay private. No laboratory measurement feature, scientific pollution percentage or suitability engine is in scope.

`WaterBody::PUBLIC_CATEGORIES` defines the map's lake/river/waterfall scope. `REPORT_CATEGORIES` includes Natural Springs / Spring Resorts for database-generated report choices and matching server validation. Selections and text survive errors; native selects, grouped no-JavaScript fallback and mobile layouts remain accessible. Twenty-six photographs and two placeholders appear publicly. Nine optimized Google Doc photographs are used under the project owner's explicit ownership/permission confirmation; source credits, unidentified-author labels and the permission declaration are retained. All twenty prior image files/credits remain intact. See [IMAGE_INVENTORY.md](IMAGE_INVENTORY.md).

Run `php artisan waterfalls:import --no-interaction` for only the two reviewed missing falls; it never runs migrations or overwrites existing metadata and refuses unexpected databases/alias conflicts. Full ExplorerSeeder fixture imports include the additive waterfall catalogue. Source points retain explicit photo/community-reference accuracy limits; no new fish records, environmental measurements or pollution reports were created.

- **Implemented Day 1:** schema/model alignment, sourced geographic seed data, safe repeatable seeding, isolated tests, and project documentation.
- **Implemented Day 2:** public Blade/Tailwind lake explorer at `/map`, database-driven Leaflet markers, OpenStreetMap basemap, responsive selection/details, public-field filtering, and desktop/mobile browser checks. `/` redirects to the explorer.
- **Implemented Day 3:** sourced historical Nile tilapia aquaculture records for the three lakes, general FAO habitat information, nullable study metadata, idempotent fish seeding, and responsive fish cards integrated with map selection. Occurrence is separate from unassessed compatibility.
- **Implemented Day 4:** public reporting form at `/reports/create`, validated/throttled submissions at `POST /reports`, server-assigned pending status, optional explicit incident points, private photos/contact details and generic confirmation. Reports are not publicly readable. Existing schema and research data are unchanged.
- **Constraints:** modify only this project; persistent database work is restricted to `aquamap_laguna`. Never reset databases, expose/change credentials, or fabricate measurements, pollution reports, or habitat conclusions.
- **Implemented Day 5:** administrator-only session login, protected dashboard/counts, report search/filters/pagination/details, private evidence streaming, controlled status transitions and atomic audit history. New admin accounts require the interactive local `admin:create` command; no public registration or community accounts exist. Individual reports stay private; the environmental refocus publishes only per-water-body totals and category counts of currently verified reports.
- **Implemented local anti-spam:** anonymous reports now have a hidden honeypot and an encrypted, session-bound 3-second server timer (2-hour validity). The existing 5-attempts/hour/IP limiter remains unchanged. Recent same-session lake/type repeats receive a nonblocking warning after saving privately as pending; other people's report history is never queried for warnings.
- **Implemented GIS expansion:** category/search/count filters, source-checked points/polygons/river reaches, compact five-tab profile dialog, 20 attributed local images, verified-report aggregates and idempotent import. Eight original images and ten prior additions are preserved; twelve additions use a read-only supplement without database imports. Bunga Twin Falls and Hidden Valley Springs now have verified CC BY 2.0 photographs. Eight profiles retain placeholders; [IMAGE_INVENTORY.md](IMAGE_INVENTORY.md) records permissions and gaps. Only licenses permitting commercial reuse are accepted. The dialog uses half the map width on desktop with bounded dimensions, mobile viewport margins, keyboard-accessible segmented tabs and verified community observations and their limits. Recovery checkpoints are in [EXPANSION_PROGRESS.md](EXPANSION_PROGRESS.md). Three resorts remain withheld; independent scientific/spring-source evidence and full river geometry remain incomplete.
- **Planned Day 6:** confirm the final integration/demo brief and deployment/recovery requirements. No measurement ingestion, automatic notifications or suitability engine exists. Original journal-point datum and positional precision remain unverified and visibly qualified; the expansion maps separate source-checked WGS84 geometry, without claiming survey accuracy. See [RETENTION_POLICY.md](RETENTION_POLICY.md) for the prototype's manual privacy/retention process; no automatic deletion is implemented.
